# Model Tenancy Act 2021 & Rental Dispute Rules

## Section 11 — Security Deposit Regulations
1. The security deposit to be paid by the tenant in advance shall not exceed:
   - Two months' rent in case of residential premises.
   - Six months' rent in case of non-residential premises.
2. The security deposit shall be refunded to the tenant on the date of handing over vacant possession of the premise after deducting valid dues/damages agreed upon in writing.
3. If the landlord fails to refund the deposit within 30 days of receiving physical possession, the tenant is entitled to claim interest or approach the Rent Authority under Section 30.

## Section 15 — Tenant Eviction & Maintenance
1. Landlords cannot cut off essential power or water supplies under any circumstances.
2. Deductions for normal wear and tear are strictly prohibited.
