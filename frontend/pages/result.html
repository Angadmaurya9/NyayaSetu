Tenant–Landlord Security Deposit Dispute

Official Source Support: Verified

YOUR PROBLEM

You vacated your rented premises after serving notice. However, your landlord has not refunded the security deposit within the mandatory statutory period.

WHAT THIS MAY INVOLVE

Under standard rental regulations and Model Tenancy Act principles, security deposits must be returned upon physical possession handover, minus documented repair costs specified in writing.

Statutory Provision: Security deposit deductions must be itemized in writing within 30 days. Unreasonable or non-itemized retention is impermissible.
HIGH PRIORITY ACTION

YOUR NEXT STEPS

01

Keep Rental Agreement & Deposit Proof

Organize bank payment receipts, lease agreement copy, and vacate notice.

02

Issue Formal Demand Notice

Send a written notice giving 7 working days to process deposit refund.

03

Escalate to Rent Controller / Consumer Forum

File a formal petition with the local Rent Controller / District Authority.
