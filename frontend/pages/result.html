Tenant–Landlord Deposit Dispute

Strong Source Support

What We Understand

You vacated your rented premises after serving the required notice period. However, your landlord has not refunded the security deposit within the agreed timeline.

What This May Involve

Under standard rental regulations and Model Tenancy Act guidelines, security deposits must be returned upon physical possession handover, minus documented repair costs.

Your Next Steps

1

Issue Formal Demand Notice

Send a written notice giving 7 days to process deposit refund.

2

Gather Evidence Checklist

Compile receipts, lease copy, and communication records.

3

Escalate to Rent Controller

File a complaint with the local Rent Controller / District Authority.

Documents to Keep

  • Rent agreement copy
  • Security deposit payment receipt / bank transaction statement
  • Written move-out communication and handover receipts

Verified Official Sources

Model Tenancy Act 2021 Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs

Section / Reference: Section 11 (Security Deposit Standards)

"The security deposit to be paid by the tenant shall be refunded upon vacating the premises after necessary deductions."
